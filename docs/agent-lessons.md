# Agent lessons

A short log of lessons that would change how a future agent works on a *different* task. Read entries relevant to your task; do not read this end to end by habit.

## Record a lesson only if

- it is likely to recur (seen at least twice, or clearly structural), and
- it is specific and actionable, and
- it is not already covered by a doc, skill, or ADR.

## Do not record

- One-off mistakes, typos, or a bad tool call.
- Narration of what happened in a task.
- Anything that belongs in a focused doc or skill: put it there instead and delete the entry once promoted.
- Preferences with no observed cost when ignored.

## Entry format

```markdown
### Short imperative title
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Area: frontend | backend | api | data | quality | workflow
- Situation: when this applies (one line)
- Lesson: what to do or avoid (one or two lines)
- Evidence: what went wrong or was found, with a file/PR reference
- Promote to: doc/skill it should move into if it recurs
```

## Maintenance

Review when adding an entry: merge duplicates, promote proven entries to their destination doc or skill, delete stale ones.
